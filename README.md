# GenAI Data Analytics – Geldium

A GenAI-powered data analytics project focused on **customer delinquency prediction, risk profiling, and AI-driven collections strategy**.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The goal of this project was to analyze customer financial data, identify factors associated with delinquency, develop model logic for predicting missed payments, and recommend an AI-driven approach for collections.

## 📊 Project Work

### 1. Exploratory Data Analysis

Analyzed a dataset containing **500 customer records** and key variables including:

- Income
- Loan Balance
- Credit Score
- Credit Utilization
- Debt-to-Income Ratio
- Missed Payments
- Employment Status
- Delinquent Account

The EDA identified missing values in Income, Loan Balance, and Credit Score, along with inconsistent employment labels and unusual credit-utilization values above 100%. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0} :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

### 2. Delinquency Prediction Model Logic

Designed a predictive modeling approach using **Logistic Regression** to estimate the likelihood of customer delinquency.

Key features considered included:

- Missed Payments
- Credit Score
- Credit Utilization
- Debt-to-Income Ratio
- Loan Balance

### 3. Predictive Insights & Recommendations

Developed a targeted collections recommendation based on customer risk indicators, with the aim of supporting proactive customer outreach.

### 4. Agentic AI Collections Strategy

Developed a workflow showing how AI can support risk assessment, customer prioritization, outreach recommendations, human review, and feedback-driven improvement.

## 🔍 Key Risk Indicators

The analysis identified the following potential delinquency risk indicators:

- Higher number of missed payments
- Lower credit scores
- High credit utilization
- Higher debt-to-income ratios
- Higher loan balances
- Employment status

These indicators were identified as relevant factors for delinquency prediction. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
